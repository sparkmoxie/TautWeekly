# Community code of conduct

TautWeekly for Plex welcomes constructive participation from people of every background
and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Protect privacy: remove credentials, personal data, and private infrastructure
  details from examples, logs, screenshots, and issue reports.
- Accept feedback in good faith and help newcomers understand project safety
  boundaries.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses and trademarks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub reporting
features. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents TautWeekly for Plex.
